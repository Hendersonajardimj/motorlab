ENGINEERING NOTES

What this model says—and what it doesn’t.

This is an illustrative first-order speed model, not a measured motor or a physical digital twin.

dω/dt = (200u − ω − d) / 0.65
e = r − ω
u = clamp(r/200 + 0.003e + 0.009∫e dt, 0, umax)
ω, r
Measured and target speed, rpm.
u
Normalized drive, 0 to the selected ceiling. Not voltage or current.
d
Equivalent speed loss, rpm. A teaching surrogate for load, not torque in N·m.
200
Assumed no-load gain, rpm per unit drive.
0.65
Assumed time constant, seconds.
Controller
PI with feedforward; gains in drive/rpm and drive/(rpm·s). Conditional integration prevents windup at the drive ceiling.
Numerics
Euler integration every 0.01 s. Each 12 s run applies the same load from 4–9 s. Speeds are clamped at zero; reverse motion is excluded.

Read the results honestly

Under sustained load, the maximum steady speed is 200umax − d. If the target is higher, more feedback cannot supply missing drive. This model omits electrical dynamics, friction detail, sensor quantization, delay, noise, thermal limits and real motor identification. Encoder speed is ideal here.

Rotation is shown at 0.2× real angular speed so the mechanism stays legible. The 3D parts are schematic geometry, not manufacturer CAD. Exploded positions explain parts; they are not operating positions.
